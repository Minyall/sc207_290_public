Domestic cats (Felis catus) are small carnivorous mammals bred for companionship and pest control. They exhibit territorial behavior, strong grooming habits, and a reliance on both auditory and visual cues for communication.

Dogs (Canis familiaris) were among the first animals domesticated by humans. They vary widely in size and temperament across more than 300 recognized breeds, fulfilling roles from assistance work to emotional support.

Goldfish (Carassius auratus) are freshwater fish originating from East Asia. They are social animals that thrive in well-oxygenated tanks and can live for over a decade under proper care.

Rabbits (Oryctolagus cuniculus domesticus) are herbivorous mammals known for their reproductive rate and need for constant dental wear through chewing fibrous foods like hay.

Guinea pigs (Cavia porcellus) are social rodents native to the Andes but fully domesticated for household environments. They communicate through a range of vocalizations including squeaks, chirps, and purr-like sounds.

A submarine is a naval vessel capable of independent underwater operation, primarily used for reconnaissance, deterrence, or combat missions. Its pressure hull allows it to withstand extreme depths.

Modern attack submarines use nuclear or diesel-electric propulsion systems. Nuclear variants can operate for months without surfacing, limited mainly by crew endurance and food supply.

Ballistic missile submarines (SSBNs) form a key component of nuclear deterrence strategies, often carrying intercontinental-range missiles with multiple warheads.

Submarine sonar systems use sound waves to detect, identify, and track other vessels. Active sonar emits pulses, while passive sonar listens for external noise signatures.

Crew members aboard submarines follow rigid rotational schedules due to confined living spaces and the absence of natural light, requiring strict psychological and physical conditioning.
